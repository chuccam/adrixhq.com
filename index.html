B2B Pricing

One app. Clear control over your B2B prices.

  • Company pricing

    Give each B2B company its own discount, and set a different rule for any of its branches.

  • Product groups

    Turn collections into price groups, up to 50, and give each company a rate per group.

  • Volume breaks

    Give bigger orders a deeper discount. Each cart line gets the rate for the quantity ordered.

How it works

From company to checkout.

  1. Choose a company

    Open one of your Shopify B2B companies. The app lists them all inside your Shopify admin.

  2. Set pricing rules

    Add a default discount, rates per product group, volume breaks or branch rules, then save.

  3. Preview the price

    Check what the company pays before any buyer sees it. Buyers get that price in the cart and at checkout.
