Why we exist

Selling across borders got harder in 2026. The US ended de minimis, so every parcel now needs a proper HS code, and Shopify opened B2B to every paid plan without giving smaller stores the tools to run it. Those gaps cost merchants real money, quietly, one order at a time.

We pick one of those gaps, build an app that closes it, and keep the app narrow enough to do that one job well.

What we believe

  • Nothing changes without you. Our apps suggest; you approve. Every change can be undone.
  • Show the source. Anything we tell you about tariffs or taxes links to the official text.
  • Minimal data. The fewest permissions the job needs, and deletion when you uninstall.
