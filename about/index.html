Why we exist

In April 2026 Shopify opened B2B to every paid plan, but left smaller stores without the tools to run it: no price for a single named customer, and three shared catalogs at most. Those gaps cost merchants real money, quietly, one order at a time.

We pick one of those gaps, build an app that closes it, and keep the app narrow enough to do that one job well.

What we believe

  • See it before it applies. Our apps show what a change will do before it is saved.
  • Say what it really does. Every limit we state is the limit the code enforces.
  • Minimal data. The fewest permissions the job needs, and deletion when you uninstall.
