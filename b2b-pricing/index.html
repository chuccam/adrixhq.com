The problem

B2B is on your plan. Per-customer prices are not.

Shopify opened B2B to Basic, Grow and Advanced in April 2026. Two limits follow, and we reproduced both on a Grow store.

No catalog for one company

A catalog assigned to a company location cannot be set to active on these plans, so no single customer gets their own price.

Three catalogs, shared

B2B markets share at most three active catalogs: price levels for groups of customers, not a rate per customer.

The workarounds cost margin

Put a customer on the wrong price level, turn them away, or hand-edit every order.

Features

Everything a wholesale price list needs.

Only buyers signed in to a company account get wholesale prices. Everyone else pays your normal price.

Company list

Find a company and price it in place

Search, filter and sort your B2B companies, open one in a side panel, and edit its pricing in a window without leaving the list.

  • Filter by pricing, label, price group or pricing type
  • Sort by name, highest discount, newest customer or most branches
  • The panel shows the company's rates, breaks and branches

Per company

Set a rate for each company

Open a company, set its discount and save. Below the form, the app shows what that customer will pay, calculated by the same function the cart runs.

  • A default discount for everything they buy, with no cap on how many companies you price
  • Your own name for the discount line, such as "Partner pricing"
  • Check every product group and quantity band before any buyer sees it

Product groups

Different rates for different products

Turn your collections into price groups, then give each company a rate per group: hardware at 40%, apparel at 15%.

  • Up to 50 groups, built from collections
  • A group's rate is final: volume breaks never cut into a low-margin group
  • The app tells you when a product moves from one group to another

Volume breaks

Deeper discounts for bigger orders

Add quantity breaks to any company. Each cart line gets the rate for the quantity ordered.

  • Up to 20 breaks per rule
  • Only the highest break the quantity reaches applies
  • Works together with the default discount

Branches

Rules for each location

A company with several locations can price each one differently, or exclude one entirely.

  • A branch rule replaces the company rule
  • "Pays retail" for a customer's own retail outlet
  • Everything else inherits the company's pricing

Safeguards

Warnings before a mistake costs money

When you save, the app checks your rules and says, in plain words, what would go wrong.

  • Volume breaks that charge big orders more, or never apply
  • Companies nobody can buy as yet: no contact with a role
  • Rates that stack on a Shopify catalog, and a discount switched off

CSV

Bring every customer in at once

Export every company's pricing, edit it in Excel or Google Sheets, and paste it back. The app shows exactly what will change before anything is saved.

  • Import up to 500 rows at a time
  • Column names are checked, so a deleted column cannot shift values
  • Nothing is written unless the preview still matches your store

How it works

Price your first customer in a few minutes.

  1. Install from the Shopify App Store

    The app opens in your Shopify admin and lists your B2B companies.

  2. Set a company's rate

    Click a company and choose Edit pricing. Add group rates, volume breaks or branch rules if you need them, then save.

  3. Check it

    Read the preview table, or create a draft order for the company with "Apply all eligible automatic discounts" ticked.

    Export, fill in your other companies, and import the file back.

FAQ

Before you install.

Which Shopify plans does it work on?

Basic, Grow and Advanced, the plans where Shopify will not activate a catalog for a single company. You need Shopify's B2B features turned on, with your customers set up as companies. The app follows your admin's language: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil) or Vietnamese.

Where do buyers see the wholesale price?

In the cart and at checkout, as a discount on each line, named "Wholesale pricing" or a label you choose. Product pages keep showing your regular price.

Does it change my product prices?

No. Your products keep their prices. Rules are saved as metafields on your companies and products, and applied while the cart is calculated.

Does it combine with my other discounts?

No. Shopify applies whichever discount is better for the buyer, not both. A buyer on 37.5% wholesale pricing who also qualifies for a 30% promotion keeps 37.5%; with 10% wholesale pricing, the 30% promotion wins. A wholesale buyer never pays more than a retail customer would.

Can it raise prices for some customers?

No. The app only takes a percentage off. To charge a company more than retail, change the product price itself.

Does it work on draft orders?

Yes, when you tick "Apply all eligible automatic discounts" on the draft order. Without it Shopify applies no automatic discount at all.

I already use a catalog with a price list. What happens?

The two stack: the app's rate comes off whatever the line already costs. 20% in a catalog plus 30% here means the buyer pays 56% of retail, not 50%. The app warns you on the company's screen when this applies.

What happens when I uninstall?

Shopify keeps an app's metafields after it is removed, so the app has a button that clears every rule, group and logo it saved. Use it before uninstalling. See the privacy policy.
