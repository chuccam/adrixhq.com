The problem

B2B is on your plan. Per-customer prices are not.

Shopify opened B2B to Basic, Grow and Advanced in April 2026. Two limits follow, and we reproduced both on a Grow store.

No catalog for one company

A catalog assigned to a company location cannot be set to active on these plans, so no single customer gets their own price.

Three catalogs, shared

B2B markets share at most three active catalogs: price levels for groups of customers, not a rate per customer.

The workarounds cost margin

Put a customer on the wrong price level, turn them away, or hand-edit every order.

FAQ

Before you install.

Which Shopify plans does it work on?

Basic, Grow and Advanced, the plans where Shopify will not activate a catalog for a single company. You need Shopify's B2B features turned on, with your customers set up as companies. The app follows your admin's language: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil) or Vietnamese.

Where do buyers see the wholesale price?

In the cart and at checkout, as a discount on each line, named "Wholesale pricing" or a label you choose. Product pages keep showing your regular price.

Does it change my product prices?

No. Your products keep their prices. Rules are saved as metafields on your companies and products, and applied while the cart is calculated.

Does it combine with my other discounts?

No. Shopify applies whichever discount is better for the buyer, not both. A buyer on 37.5% wholesale pricing who also qualifies for a 30% promotion keeps 37.5%; with 10% wholesale pricing, the 30% promotion wins. A wholesale buyer never pays more than a retail customer would.

Can it raise prices for some customers?

No. The app only takes a percentage off. To charge a company more than retail, change the product price itself.

Does it work on draft orders?

Yes, when you tick "Apply all eligible automatic discounts" on the draft order. Without it Shopify applies no automatic discount at all.

I already use a catalog with a price list. What happens?

The two stack: the app's rate comes off whatever the line already costs. 20% in a catalog plus 30% here means the buyer pays 56% of retail, not 50%. The app warns you on the company's screen when this applies.

What happens when I uninstall?

Shopify keeps an app's metafields after it is removed, so the app has a button that clears every rule, group and logo it saved. Use it before uninstalling. See the privacy policy.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan and no charge through Shopify.
