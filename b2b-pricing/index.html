Adrix B2B Pricing · for Shopify · Free

A wholesale price for every company. No Plus plan.

On Basic, Grow and Advanced, Shopify will not activate a catalog assigned to a company location, so you cannot give one named B2B customer their own price. Adrix B2B Pricing stores the rate on the company itself and applies it at checkout, for as many companies as you have.

Shopify B2B stops at the fourth customer.

Features

Wholesale pricing that stays out of retail.

Only buyers signed in to a company account get wholesale prices. Everyone else pays your normal price.

A rate per company

Set a base discount for each B2B company, for as many companies as you have.

Rates per product group

Build groups from your collections, so tools and consumables can sell to the same buyer at different rates.

Volume breaks

Up to 20 quantity breaks per rule. Each cart line gets the rate for the quantity ordered.

Rules per branch

Give one location of a company its own rate, or have it pay full price, such as the customer's own retail outlet.

Your own label

Buyers see the discount as "Wholesale pricing", or a name you choose, such as "Trade price".

CSV in and out

Export every company's pricing, edit it in a spreadsheet, and import up to 500 rows at a time.

Setup

Price your first customer in a few minutes.

  1. Install from the Shopify App Store

    The app opens inside your Shopify admin and lists your B2B companies.

  2. Pick a company and set its rate

    Add group rates, volume breaks or branch rules if you need them.

  3. Bring the rest in by CSV

    Export, fill in the rates for your other companies, and import the file back.

  4. Buyers see it in the cart

    Pricing runs inside Shopify's own checkout as a Shopify Function. Nothing is added to your theme.

FAQ

Before you install.

Do I need Shopify Plus?

No. The app is built for Basic, Grow and Advanced, where Shopify will not activate a catalog for a single company.

Where do buyers see the wholesale price?

In the cart and at checkout, as a discount on each line. Product pages keep showing your regular price.

Does it change my product prices?

No. Your products keep their prices. Rules are saved as metafields on your companies and products, and applied while the cart is calculated.

I already use a catalog with a price list. What happens?

The two stack: the app's rate comes off whatever the line already costs. 20% in a catalog plus 30% here means the buyer pays 56% of retail, not 50%. The app warns you on the company's screen when this applies.

What happens when I uninstall?

The app can remove every rule it saved before you uninstall, so nothing is left in your store. See the privacy policy for what we store.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan and no charge through Shopify.
