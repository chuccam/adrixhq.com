Adrix B2B Pricing · for Shopify · Free

A wholesale price for every company. No Plus plan.

On Basic, Grow and Advanced, Shopify will not activate a catalog assigned to a company location, so you cannot give one named B2B customer their own price. Adrix B2B Pricing stores the rate on the company itself and applies it at checkout, for as many companies as you have.

Features

Wholesale pricing that stays out of retail.

Only buyers signed in to a company account see wholesale prices. Everyone else pays your normal price.

A rate per company

Set a base discount for each B2B company. There is no cap on how many companies you price.

Rates per product group

Group products from your collections, so tools and consumables can sell to the same buyer at different rates.

Volume breaks

Each cart line gets the rate for the quantity ordered, worked out at checkout.

Rules per branch

Give one location of a company its own rate, or have it pay full price.

Setup

Price your first customer in a few minutes.

  1. Install from the Shopify App Store

    The app opens inside your Shopify admin and lists your B2B companies.

  2. Pick a company and set its rate

    Add group rates or volume breaks if you need them.

  3. Bring the rest in by CSV

    Export every company's pricing to a CSV file, edit it in a spreadsheet, and import it back.

  4. Leave nothing behind

    Pricing lives in metafields on your own companies and products. The app can remove all of it before you uninstall.
