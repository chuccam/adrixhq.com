Why the review cart showed no wholesale discount

Adrix B2B Pricing · October 1, 2026

The cart had no B2B company attached, so there was no company rate to apply. The discount itself works: below is what our function received on the review store, and the same pricing in the cart and checkout of a buyer who is signed in as a company contact.

What the function received on the review store

Shopify runs the app’s discount function whenever a cart changes. On the review store it ran 51 times on September 29, and every run received buyerIdentity.purchasingCompany: null, or no buyer identity at all. A cart without a purchasing company is a retail cart, and the app leaves retail prices alone, so each run correctly returned no discount.

Dev Dashboard → Adrix B2B Pricing → Logs, one of the 51 runs. The store’s domain and ID are blurred.

When Shopify attaches a company to a cart

Shopify B2B sends a cart as a company’s only when both of these hold:

  1. The store uses customer accounts, not legacy customer accounts (Settings → Customer accounts). Shopify does not support B2B buyers on legacy accounts.
  2. The customer signs in as a contact of the company who has a role at one of its locations: Ordering only or Location admin. Signing in with an email that has no role at a location is a retail sign-in.

Otherwise the buyer shops as a retail customer and Shopify sends the cart without a company. No app can apply company pricing to that cart.

The same pricing for a contact with a role

Our Grow demo store, company Summit Wholesale at 20% with the label “Partner pricing”, buyer signed in as its contact with the Ordering only role. Ten snowboards at $749.95 cost $599.96 each, and checkout shows total savings of $1,499.90.

How to test it

  1. In the app, open a company and set a default discount, for example 20%.
  2. Check Settings → Customer accounts is set to customer accounts, not legacy.
  3. In Shopify admin, go to Customers → Companies → that company, add a customer as a contact, and give them the role Ordering only at a location.
  4. Sign in to the storefront as that customer (choose the location if Shopify asks) and add a product. The cart and checkout show the discount with the company’s label.
  5. Or, in Shopify admin, create a draft order for that company and tick Apply all eligible automatic discounts. Without that box Shopify applies no automatic discount to draft orders.

What we changed in the app

The app now names both causes on screen, so a merchant, or a reviewer, sees why a rate is not reaching a cart before testing one:

  • On a store that uses legacy customer accounts, the company list and every company screen say “B2B buyers cannot sign in to this store yet”, with a link to Settings → Customer accounts.
  • On a company none of whose contacts can order, the company screen says “Nobody can buy as [company] yet”, with a link to the company in Shopify admin.
